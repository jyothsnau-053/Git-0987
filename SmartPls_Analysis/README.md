# Digital Self-Efficacy Mediation in Digital Leadership
### A PLS-SEM Analysis of ICT Preparedness and Employee-AI Support in the Indian IT Sector

## About this project
This is an independent academic research project conducted by an MBA (Human Resources)graduate, examining how Employer Preparedness for ICT and AI Support influence Sustainable Digital Leadership in the Indian IT sector, with Digital Self-Efficacy tested as a mediating mechanism.

## Abstract
This study examines how Employer Preparedness for ICT and AI Support influence Sustainable Digital Leadership within the Indian IT sector, with Digital Self-Efficacy tested as a mediating mechanism. Data were collected from 384 employees across five major IT firms — Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Cognizant, and Accenture India — using a validated Likert-scale questionnaire, and analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modelling (PLS-SEM) in SmartPLS. Results show that both ICT preparedness and AI Support significantly influence Sustainable Digital Leadership, both directly and indirectly through Digital Self-Efficacy. All seven hypotheses were supported.

**Keywords:** Sustainable Digital Leadership, Digital Self-Efficacy, Employer Preparedness for ICT, AI Support, PLS-SEM, Indian IT Sector

## Conceptual Model
Employer Preparedness for ICT and AI Support → Digital Self-Efficacy (mediator) → Sustainable Digital Leadership

## Methodology
- **Design**: Quantitative, cross-sectional survey
- **Sample**: 384 employees across Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Cognizant, and Accenture India
- **Sampling method**: Stratified random sampling; sample size calculated using Cochran's formula (95% confidence, 5% margin of error)
- **Instrument**: 37-item questionnaire across 4 constructs, 5-point Likert scale. All items were adapted from validated scales published in Scopus-indexed journals.
- **Analysis tool**: SmartPLS (PLS-SEM)

## Key Findings
- All 7 hypotheses were statistically supported
- AI Support was the strongest direct predictor of Sustainable Digital Leadership
- Employer Preparedness for ICT was the strongest predictor of Digital Self-Efficacy
- The model explained 60.9% of the variance (R²) in Sustainable Digital Leadership
- Digital Self-Efficacy was confirmed as a significant mediating mechanism

## Data & Ethics
Data was collected with informed consent from voluntary participants across the five organizations studied. No personally identifiable information (names, emails, or contact details) was collected or is included in this repository. The shared dataset contains only anonymized, item-level numeric responses used in the statistical analysis.
