## General Notes

- Total sample size: N = 100, no missing data across analyzed variables.
- Stream sample sizes are unequal: BSc (N=3), Bcom (N=47), BA (N=9), BBA (N=41). Any Stream-based comparisons involving BSc carry low statistical power and should be interpreted cautiously.
- Year sample sizes: I Year (N=15), II Year (N=7), III Year (N=78).
- Items with a binary/nominal value structure (mostly Q19 series) were tested via Chi-square/crosstabs only; ANOVA was not applied to these, as they are not ordinal scales.
- An awareness composite across the Q19 tool-awareness items was considered but not included in the final analysis, as the scoring approach could not be consistently justified across items with differing category structures (e.g., Q19.8 has three categories versus binary for the rest).
